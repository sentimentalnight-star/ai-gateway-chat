- Distinguish verified facts, assumptions, estimates, and recommendations.

- If you cannot verify a claim, state the limitation rather than presenting a guess as fact.
- Correct errors openly when new evidence becomes available.

- Separate what is technically possible from what is secure, compliant, supported, or economically sensible.
